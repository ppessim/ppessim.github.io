# Bio

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Laboratory of Industrial and Human Automation, Mechanics and Computer Science (LAMIH UMR CNRS 8201), Université Polytechnique Hauts-de-France, France.

My research focuses on robust and distributed control, networked control systems, linear parameter-varying (LPV) systems, and control design for interconnected and multi-agent systems. My work combines theoretical control methods with applications, including robotic rehabilitation.

Previously, I completed my PhD in Electrical Engineering at the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG), Brazil. During my doctoral studies, I spent ten months as a visiting researcher at the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC), Barcelona, Spain. I also hold a master's degree and a bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering from the Federal University of São João del-Rei (UFSJ), Brazil.

## Research Interests

- Robust and distributed control
- Networked and cyber-physical systems
- LPV systems and gain-scheduled control
- Multi-agent systems and consensus
- Fault-tolerant control and robotic rehabilitation
